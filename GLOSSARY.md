# Claude Widget

A macOS menu bar app that mirrors Claude Code sessions and hangs a hub of quick Mac tools from the notch.

## Language

### Surfaces

**Notch**:
The black cutout at the top of a MacBook screen, used as the app's front door.

**Critter**:
The animated Claude figure in the menu bar; the hub's door on screens without a notch.
_Avoid_: status icon, mascot

**Hub**:
The Liquid Glass panel that drops from the notch or the critter and holds the modules.
_Avoid_: popover, island, dropdown

**Pill**:
A small glass capsule under the notch or the critter that shows one thing (an alert, a conversion) without opening the hub.
_Avoid_: toast, banner, peek

**Strip**:
The black band that widens the closed notch while music plays.
_Avoid_: now-playing bar

### Convert

**Conversion**:
Turning dropped photos or recordings into another format, saved as new files.
_Avoid_: export, transcode

**Target**:
A glass tile under the notch, one per format, that files are dropped onto to choose that format.
_Avoid_: drop zone, button

**Photo**:
A still image in a kind the app converts: HEIC, HEIF, WebP, AVIF, PNG, JPEG or TIFF.
_Avoid_: image (broader)

**Recording**:
A QuickTime movie with a video track, such as a screen recording.
_Avoid_: video, clip

**Original**:
The file that was dragged in, when the drag came with one. It is never changed.
_Avoid_: source file, input

**Fileless drop**:
A drop that came with no file on disk: a promise from Photos or the screenshot thumbnail, or raw image data from a browser.

**Result**:
A file a conversion saved.
_Avoid_: output
